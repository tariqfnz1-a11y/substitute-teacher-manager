📌 Administrative Guidelines

  • Check the actual timetable before assigning a substitute.
  • Do not assign a teacher who already has a class in that period.
  • Consider the teacher's existing substitution workload.
  • Verify class, subject and period details before approval.
  • Approve and save the final plan for record keeping.
